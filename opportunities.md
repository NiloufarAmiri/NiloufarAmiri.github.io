  <h2 class="opportunity-project-title">Bio-Inspired Soft Robotics</h2>
  <p>We are seeking a <strong>motivated undergraduate volunteer research assistant</strong> to contribute to an exciting project involving a <strong>bio-inspired soft robot</strong> at the Robotics, Mechatronics and Automation Laboratory (RMAL), Toronto Metropolitan University.</p>
  <a class="opportunity-apply-link" href="mailto:ni.amiri@torontomu.ca?subject=Volunteer%20Research%20Assistant%20Application">Apply by email <span aria-hidden="true">↗</span></a>

The selected candidate will contribute to experimental setup development, hardware prototyping, experimental testing, data collection, and technical documentation.

This opportunity offers **continuous research mentorship, hands-on experience in soft robotics, exposure to multidisciplinary research**, and the potential to contribute to research publications and present findings at leading conferences.

## Qualifications

- Currently enrolled in an undergraduate program in Mechanical Engineering, Electrical Engineering, Mechatronics Engineering, or a related discipline.
- Strong motivation, curiosity, and willingness to learn.
- Availability to work in person at RMAL, Toronto Metropolitan University, 2–3 times per week.
- Familiarity with mechanical prototyping, fabrication, or 3D printing.
- Experience with experimental testing, instrumentation, or data acquisition.
- Basic proficiency in Python programming and data analysis.

## Preferred Qualifications

- Strong independent thinking and problem-solving skills.
- Previous experience with embedded systems, sensors, or data acquisition systems.
- Familiarity with robot mechanics and robotic systems.

**Previous research experience is not required.** Upper-year undergraduate students with relevant technical skills are particularly encouraged to apply.

## Position Details

- **Type:** Volunteer research position
- **Location:** RMAL, Toronto Metropolitan University
- **Time commitment:** 10 hours per week, in person (2–3 days per week)
- **Expected duration:** 3 months
- **Application deadline:** November 15, 2026

<p class="opportunity-compensation"><strong>Compensation:</strong> This position is initially voluntary. Depending on research performance and funding availability, financial compensation may be considered at a later stage but is not guaranteed.</p>

## How to Apply

Interested applicants are encouraged to send their **CV** to **[ni.amiri@torontomu.ca](mailto:ni.amiri@torontomu.ca?subject=Volunteer%20Research%20Assistant%20Application)** by **November 15, 2026**.
